Solmar Streets is an open-world crime game set in Solmar, a sun-soaked coastal city. Jax Moreno returns home in debt to Mama Rosa, and takes her jobs across the city while Detective Voss and the Solmar PD hunt him. The game has a procedural city to explore on foot and by car, traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal, a five-star wanted system with pursuit cars, roadblocks and a police helicopter, a twelve-mission story chain (including timed runs and a final pier shootout), pistol, SMG and shotgun combat, an NOS boost for cars, a three-station synthesised car radio, a chase camera, beachfront shops and a safehouse, a day/night cycle with a HUD clock, a HUD with a live minimap, synthesised sound and music, and a save that keeps your progress.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.

Controls: WASD move, Shift sprint (NOS boost while driving), mouse drag to look, E enter or leave a car, F punch, click to shoot (after the first job), 1/2/3 switch weapon, R reload, Q car radio, C chase camera, 4/5/6 buy ammo at the gun shop, 7 heal and 8 NOS refill at the clinic, H lay low at the safehouse, Space handbrake, P pause, M mute. Everything runs offline from static files; three.js is vendored under `game/vendor/`.
